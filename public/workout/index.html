Day 7 · Sunday
Rest — Cardio Only
Cardio

Incline treadmill — 4.5–5.5 km/h, 8–12% incline, 20 minutes. No lifting today.

Weeks 1–4

Cap ALL exercises at 3 working sets regardless of what's listed. Let your joints and tendons adapt. It will feel easy — that's the point.

Deload

Every 4–6 weeks, reduce weight by 40% and sets by half. Don't wait until you feel beat up. Schedule it in advance.

Recomp Rule

Moderate calorie deficit (200–300 kcal). Protein at 1g per lb bodyweight. 7–10k steps most days. Diet and walking do the fat loss — lifting preserves the muscle.

Effort

Days 1–3 compounds at RPE 8 (leave ~2 reps). Isolation at RPE 9. Every 2nd-hit compound at RPE 7. Thursday at RPE 6 — leave the gym fresher. Never fail squat, deadlift, or bench. Rest 3 min on compounds, ~90s on isolation. Session cap 70–90 min.

Progressive Overload

Add weight only when you hit the top of the rep range on all sets. Track every session. Small plates (1.25–2.5kg) are your best friends. Miss two weeks in a row — drop 5–10% or switch the variation. Don't add junk volume.

Daily Cardio

Incline treadmill, 4km/h at 10% incline, 10 minutes — every lifting day (Mon–Sat). On squat days (Wed, Sat), do this before lifting if possible, not right after. Sunday: 4.5–5.5km/h at 8–12% incline, 20 minutes.
