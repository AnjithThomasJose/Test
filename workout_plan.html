Weeks 1–4

Cap ALL exercises at 3 working sets regardless of what's listed. Let your joints and tendons adapt. It will feel easy — that's the point.

Deload

Every 4–6 weeks, reduce weight by 40% and sets by half. Don't wait until you feel beat up. Schedule it in advance.

Recomp Rule

Moderate calorie deficit (200–300 kcal). High protein (0.8–1g per lb bodyweight). Diet does the fat loss — lifting preserves the muscle.

Progressive Overload

Add weight only when you hit the top of the rep range on all sets. Track every session. Small plates (1.25–2.5kg) are your best friends.
